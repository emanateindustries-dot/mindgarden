The Examination

There is one examination. It has no questions. You stand in front of a termite mound, you look at it for a very long time, and then a number arrives. The number must be right.

Do this one in the bush if you can. This page is for rehearsal only, and a mound ranked here is only ranked here.

The Constitution

  1. Every mound shall be ranked.
  2. Rankings are final.
  3. Mounds are looked at, never touched. A mound owned by a named individual (see: Raff, #3) is doubly protected.
  4. The Society's motto is under review. It has been under review for some time.

Notices

  • A mound in a hat is, strictly speaking, not being looked at as a mound.
  • Do not propose a mound for promotion. Mounds are not promoted. They are discovered.
  • Cathedral termites in the Northern Territory have built mounds over seven metres tall. The Society has seen the photographs and is satisfied.

Contact the Society

There is no phone number. There is no head office. There is, reluctantly, this website. If you need the Society, find Brodie. He is in every practical sense the Society's voice.
